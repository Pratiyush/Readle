# Release notes

Play "What's new" text, one `## <version>` section per upload (the version line from
`app/pubspec.yaml`). `app/tool/release.sh` refuses to build when the section is missing or over
Play's 500 characters. English first; add other languages in the Play Console.

## 0.1.0+1
107 short illustrated German stories, A1 to B2. Tap any word to hear it and see what it means,
translate any sentence, or listen to the whole story. Then check yourself with a quick quiz and
practise your saved words with spaced repetition. Works offline. Optional daily reminder.
